# Mountain background

Photograph: **Snowy Annapurna Range – panorama**, Ali Sabbagh, 17 February 2017.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Snowy_Annapurna_Range_-_panorama.jpg

Original: https://www.flickr.com/photos/alisabbagh/34979848156/

License: **CC0 1.0**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

`mountains.bmp` is an adapted decorative panorama: skyline extraction, neutral
color treatment, resizing and a reflected wrap. It is not a geographic 360°
photograph or a location-specific depiction of the ski venues.

Regenerate with `python tools/prepare_mountain_panorama.py ORIGINAL.jpg`
(development dependency: ImageMagick). The game only loads the prepared
BMP once; it does not download or process photos.
